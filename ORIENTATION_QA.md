1. **Algorithm.** `estimateDocumentOrientation(fullTextAnnotation)` consumes original ordered word vertices, never axis-aligned OCR boxes. It ignores malformed, non-finite, non-convex, degenerate, tiny or inconsistent-edge polygons. Missing protobuf scalar coordinates mean zero. It averages the top edge and reversed bottom edge as vectors, assigns sufficiently cardinal observations to 0/90/180/270, and uses equal-word voting plus circular concentration. Off-axis observations remain in the agreement denominator. The result includes orientation, usable evidence count and consensus confidence (agreement times dominant-cluster circular concentration); this is not a calibrated probability or Google's text confidence. No OCR text is required or logged by the estimator.

Exact successful preview followed by final upload on the same server process within TTL: zero additional orientation requests. 5. **Trusted deduplication.** Bounded in-process Map, maximum 512 successful results, five-minute TTL from completion. Key binds authenticated server-side user ID, SHA-256 of exact original bytes, orientation policy version and basic-check configuration. Cache entries contain cloned decisions only, not images or OCR text. Clients cannot supply the user binding, key, policy or trusted decision. Only successful results are stored; entries expire lazily, with oldest-entry eviction at capacity. Process restart/another worker causes a safe miss and full validation. No Redis, database model or token infrastructure added. Assignment identity is not included because orientation is independent of assignment content; existing final-route ownership checks still apply.

6. **Centralized thresholds.** `ORIENTATION_POLICY`: version `vision-word-orientation-v1`; at least four agreeing usable words; horizontal edges at least 8 pixels and vertical edges at least 4 pixels on the bounded 1024-pixel analysis copy; top/bottom directional disagreement at most 20 degrees; observations within 25 degrees of a cardinal direction; dominant agreement at least 80%; dominant circular concentration at least 0.95. Four-word minimum accommodates a short sentence while avoiding a one/two-word guess. The cardinal tolerance deliberately includes ordinary 3/8/12-degree tilt; ambiguous diagonal directions remain undetermined. Geometry filters exclude unstable tiny shapes and inconsistent boxes. These are conservative initial engineering thresholds, not real-photo calibrated thresholds. Changes to algorithm/threshold semantics require a policy version bump. Resolution/exposure thresholds and calculations are unchanged.

7. **Undetermined and errors.** Required upright check passes only for determined upright. Determined 90/180/270 fails with “Please rotate the image so the writing is upright.” Weak/conflicting/absent evidence returns required-unavailable and a verify/retry message. Provider/network errors return a technical retry message, never a rotation accusation. Failed basic checks skip orientation to avoid unnecessary cost. No source image is rotated: Vision receives a PNG encoded from the existing EXIF-normalized disposable grayscale analysis copy. Original bytes remain unchanged.
