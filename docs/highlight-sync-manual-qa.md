# Manual QA: highlight sync in Review corrections

Add these to the release's manual QA checklist (teacher section). Use a real handwritten submission.

**Setup:** open a submission as a teacher, open **Review corrections**, press **Show image and transcript**.

1. **Three views agree.** Click a correction in the list, then a word in the transcript, then a badge on the image. Each time the same correction is marked in all three, and the others scroll to it.
   Pass: the marks sit on the right handwritten words. Fail: a mark is on the wrong word.
2. **Hidden and removed items.** Hide one correction and remove another, then select each.
   Pass: both are dashed or struck through and say **Approximate position**, and the mark is on the right word. Compare with what the student sees using **Student preview**: neither is there.
3. **No position.** Find a correction marked **Not located on image** (for example after a word could not be located, or an item under **Needs review**) and select it.
   Pass: it selects in the list, nothing is marked on the image, and the message says why.
4. **Keyboard only.** Using Tab, Enter and Escape, move through list rows, transcript words and image badges.
   Pass: focus is always visible, the selection follows, and Escape clears it. With a screen reader, the selected correction is announced.
5. **iPad, real device, both orientations** (needs an iPad). Tap a badge, a transcript word and a list row. Rotate the iPad with a correction half typed in the form.
   Pass: a tap selects in all three, no details sheet opens over the image, scrolling inside each panel works without moving the page, and after rotating the workspace, the selection and the typed text are all still there.
6. **Long or multi-page work.** Open a PDF or a multi-image submission, and an essay with many corrections if you have one. Select a correction on another page, then press **Hide image and transcript**.
   Pass: the image moves to that page and the workspace stays on screen, selecting feels immediate, and closing returns the page to how it was. In the browser's Network tab, opening the workspace downloads no image again.
